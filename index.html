i. The Land

A quiet end of the island, seven acres on a slope above the harbor.

Perched above the harbor on the western edge of Martha's Vineyard, Menemsha Farm spans seven acres shaped by salt air and steady work. Three generations, anchored by Joe.

We grow stone fruits — peaches and nectarines — alongside heirloom apples, cultivated honey, pasture-raised eggs, and cut flowers including our beautiful and famous tulips. Everything goes to market.

The work is quiet, mostly, and constant, and beautiful.

ii. The Harvest

The beauty of the early fall harvest is earned in the unseen work of spring.
